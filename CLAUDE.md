Working language: **English.** Reply to H in English. Short, direct, no excessive politeness.

## What this is
Chinese character trainer for adults (spaced repetition), a reinterpretation of 悟空识字 without the kids' aesthetic.
User: H, product manager, Beijing, **phone-only (Android/Xiaomi, Chrome, VPN), no computer**.
UI language: English. Learning content: Chinese + pinyin + English meaning.

## Live deployment
- Repo: `henglicam/zeichentrainer` · GitHub Pages, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
- URL: `https://henglicam.github.io/zeichentrainer/`
- Push to `main` → Pages rebuilds automatically (~1–2 min). `index.html` must stay in the repo root.

### The flow (AI-first — "nobody wants to see OCR if not necessary")
1. **Camera:** `<input type="file" capture="environment">` (Take photo) or `<input type="file" multiple>` (**From album** — several at once, all land in the inbox, the first opens in crop mode; `addPhoto` per file) → the photo is normalized (EXIF baked in, max 1600 px) and stored in `inbox`. A pulsing "Processing photo …" placeholder appears at once; the inbox re-renders on `visibilitychange`; pull-to-refresh is off (`overscroll-behavior-y: contain`).
2. **Frame:** the photo lands directly in crop mode (bright dashed frame with a dark outline, nothing dimmed, corner handles resize, dragging inside moves, preview below). **Reading starts by itself 1.2 s after the finger lifts** (`READ_TIMER`; a corner drag restarts the wait; "Read now" and a small "Image only" link remain). No dialogs in the flow: no frame → nothing happens, a duplicate text is an inline note. **The frame stays on the photo** through reading and the card preview (`cropRectStyle` re-draws it from `CROP.rect` in percentages after any re-render) and goes only on Save or Cancel; a corner drag meanwhile re-reads.
3. **Read** (`cropSign`): the framed area is **straightened first** (`deskewBlob`: projection-profile skew estimate on a ≤ 360 px copy, ±20° in 1° steps then half degrees, text pixels = far from the median brightness so both polarities count; below 1.5° nothing happens; the rotated crop becomes the card image; H: a tilted 首都铁骑 read as garbage) → on-device OCR line by line (Tesseract.js 7, `chi_sim`, PSM 6, symbols under confidence 35 dropped; `readPass`; **digits are kept** when fairly sure (confidence ≥ 70) and near the characters' height (0.4–1.6 × the line's median box height — the boxes' heights swing: a ribbon ring once read as a 1 at 2.1 ×, the real 30 of 30分钟 measured 0.55–0.7 ×; H: the number was missing from the translation); letters stay out; a number reads as itself in `lineMeaning` and stays one token in the pinyin, `pySpaced`) — the status text lives in `READING[id]` and is re-queried on every update, so a re-render (foreground return, AI answer for another photo) cannot swallow it or the error; the user sees **one line with a moving bar** (`readingHTML`: "Reading the text …") for every step, only failures show as text (H: the steps are of no use to the user); a step that lasts longer than `READ_STUCK` (20 s) shows its own text under the bar ("still at: loading the reader …", `READ_AT`), because a stuck step is a failure; `cropSign` is guarded from its first line (the cut-out, the reader load) and every failure shows as "OCR failed: …" and goes to the error log — → second look (`secondLook`) and competing readings (below) → `SIGN[id]` = `{lines, orig, conf, boxes, img, angle, tightened, region, alts}` (`img` = the straightened, maybe tightened crop the text was read from, `boxes[k][i]` = each symbol's box in it; `region.passes` = the competition and `region.tightBlob` the tight crop, for diagnosis; **`alts`** (v96) = up to six other readings, distinct texts best first, always including the traditional reader's best and the best reading of another length — the AI gets them as `alt` ("the true text is often a mix of them, or a well-known name they circle around"; 养兴多 / 义乐多 / 和准浴多 → 养乐多), the picker offers their characters at the same position (`altCharsAt`, lines with the same character count only), and they are saved on the card as `alts` for a later AI check) → the editor, laid out **like the Edit form** (H, v90): a Text field with a **character strip** and one input per line (see below), a Pinyin field and a Meaning field that follow the reading and the AI check unless edited by hand (`sg.pinTouched`/`meanTouched`, `pinEdit`/`meanEdit` — a hand-written meaning saves as verified, no offline model), a **⚑ Flag for review** checkbox with a note field (`sg.flag`/`flagNote`, `data-sflag`/`data-snote` — H, v94: flag a new card at once instead of opening it again; the Add form has the same pair, `f-flag`/`f-note`, kept in the draft), the source under the meaning ("Checked by the AI" / "from the phrasebook" / "composed word by word, unverified"); no per-line pinyin and no gloss table any more; with a red hint when only 1–2 characters were found with low confidence (frame tightly, drag a corner) → **automatic AI check** (`signAskAI`, when the AI is live) → the preview shows "Read and checked by the AI" with pinyin, meaning and the AI's note → **Save card**. One line of ≤ 4 characters becomes a word card (`t:"Custom"`, `seg`, crop image), anything longer a sign card (`kind:"sign"`, lines joined with `\n`, `segs`, `gloss`, `img` + `imgFull`). Cards checked here are stored `mt:{src:"llm",verified:true}`.
   - **Second look** (H: a Maotai label in a loose frame with stripes, ribbon and neck label was read as one false character from the ribbon): **on every reading** (v96 — until v95 only when the first pass found text in under 60 % of the frame or was unsure; H: "time is no issue", and the copies read 脊 where the first pass had 疹) that text area is cut out — its vertical band from the boxes (heights are reliable) plus half a height, its **horizontal ends from the image** (`textRowExtent`: within the band, walk the columns outward from the reader's span until a gap wider than 1.3 text heights or the edge; the boxes' ends drift and the first pass may have lost a character altogether — 骑 was cut in half once, then lost altogether) plus one text height, then 1.5 text heights of plain margin in the crop's **median colour** (the reader wants margins, real ones bring the clutter back, and a corner-sampled red ribbon once framed a white label in red) — straightened on its own and read at four character heights (45/60/75/90 px, `scales`) plus native while cheap. When a tightened reading wins, the **card image** is the same area with a real photo margin of one text height, rotated by the same angle (`deskewBlob(blob, forced)`), not the padded reading crop. Runs silently — the reading crop is not shown (H: "run it in the background, the user doesn't have to see it"; it served to diagnose the cut-off crops of v73–v74). Not yet timed on the Xiaomi.
   - **Black-on-white and chromaticity copies** (H: 业主直租, white on red behind glass, read as 本; v95: 活菌型乳酸菌乳饮品, red print on a curved cream Yakult bottle, read as nothing — and the logo 養樂多 as 夸交多 / 一汰 / 洛相本): the reader's own thresholding gives nothing on such crops. The tight crop is always read as a **black-and-white copy** (`toBW`: Otsu on the grey image, polarity so that the majority is white — reads the glass case) and as a **chromaticity copy** (`chromaCanvas`/`toChroma`: each pixel's r/(r+g+b), g/(r+g+b), distance from the median chromaticity, Otsu on that, minority = ink — shading falls out, so the bottle's shadow no longer matters; measured on the Yakult line: grey nothing at any size, chromaticity 菌型乳酸菌乳饮品 at 96 %; on black-on-white text it is noise and simply loses) at the same sizes (45/60/75/90/110 px plus native up to 200 px). **Still weak (< 180)? The traditional-character reader** (v96, `chi_tra` from tessdata_fast, 1.7 MB, a second worker made on first need by `traWorker` from the same recipe, `makeWorker`) reads all three copies at the same sizes; its lines are converted to simplified characters (`t2s`, `vendor/t2s.txt` = OpenCC's character table, 3 222 pairs) and compete like any other reading. H's Yakult logo 養樂多 is traditional: the simplified model can only answer with the nearest simplified shapes (养兴多, 和准浴多), chi_tra reads 義樂多 → 义乐多; on a simplified sign it reads the traditional forms of the same characters (專業脊柱矯正 → 专业脊柱矫正), so it never hurts. The "best" (float) models were measured too and bring nothing on these crops — not shipped. When the best reading is still weak (`WEAK_READ` 180 since v96 — the logo's garbage scored 58, nothing found is 0), the **whole frame** is read as both copies by both readers at sizes from the ink itself (`inkHeight`: the longest run of ink rows in a small chromaticity copy — the old guess from the frame height read a line filling 40 % of a loose frame at 19 px). Cost: 15 passes on the tight crop always, 15 more with the traditional reader and 12 on the frame when the reading is poor (H: "time is no issue"); the Yakult crops take 3–9 s in headless Chromium, not yet timed on the Xiaomi.
   - **Readings compete and merge:** the lines of all tight readings are **merged by vertical band** (a line's band = the median centre ± half the median height of its boxes, not their extremes — inflated boxes once spanned both lines of a two-line sign and folded them into one cluster, so the first line vanished; each reading tends to get one line right and lose another; the most confident line per band is kept, short low-confidence strays dropped) and the merged result competes with the single readings; `readingScore` = mean confidence **cubed** (cf × (cf/100)², v96: real text reads at 90–97 %, the reader's garbage at 79–86 %, and a linear weight let one more character outweigh that) × n^0.35 × fragment penalty for lines under three characters (v96: /3 — /4 punished a real three-character logo against four garbage characters) × (1 − 0.5 × share of 1–2-character lines; v95: a soup of seven short lines, n = 15, once outscored the real three characters) × (0.75 + 0.5 × dictionary word coverage — a stronger coverage weight was tried in v96 and let a garbage soup with accidental two-character words win), because the model's output swings with scale even on a clean crop (measured: the same image perfect at 0.7×, garbage at 1×); at the competition the score gets an **agreement bonus**, × (1 + 0.1 per other pass with the identical text, ≤ 1.5) (v96: 业主直租 from three passes lost a tie to 业主直祖 from one). Inside a pass, lines of tiny characters beside a tall line are dropped (`readPass`, 0.45 × the tallest line's median box height; v96: a soup of five such lines, 18 characters, outscored the logo's three). A merged band is kept only when two passes saw it or it reads at ≥ 85 %, and when its height is at least 0.45 × the tallest band (v95: the oval of the Yakult logo became 和一一 in the colour pass and rode along). **Known limit:** two characters read as one at a small scale (专业脊柱矫正 → 专业冰矫正 at 98 %) beats the right six-character reading; the LSTM's symbol boxes are evenly spaced guesses, so a fused glyph leaves no geometric trace (a box-width penalty was tried and did nothing) — the AI fixes it from the alternatives. Measured on a striped, ribboned composite: garbage at 86 % → 本区域禁止违规 at 99 %; the 12° sign still reads both lines; the Yakult screenshots read 5菌型乳酸菌乳饮品 (the faded 活 at the bottle's edge becomes a stray 5) and 养兴多 with 义乐多 among the alternatives (v96; 楽 is a Japanese glyph neither model knows — 兴/府 and 樂 are their nearest; the phone's tighter frame reads 和准兴多, the 和 being the oval's left arc; the AI is expected to make 养乐多 of it). Diagnostics: `region.passes` (the competition) and `region.tightBlob` (the tight crop).
   - **What the reader sees must be a JPEG** (`READ_JPEG` 0.95): this Tesseract build misreads large canvas PNGs and WebPs (same pixels: JPEG → the full line at 99 %, PNG → one character); intermediate crops stay PNG so only the last step is lossy — two JPEG generations in a row lost the line too. `deskewBlob` always hands back a JPEG; `jpegOf` re-encodes card images to JPEG 0.85 at save. The pad's small black-on-white images are indifferent (measured).
   - **Fixing one character** (H: "sometimes one character is wrong while the others are right"): every character of a line is a button (`charStripHTML`, low-confidence ones ringed in the tint). Tap one → picker (`openCharPick`) under the line, **two levels: suggestions, then drawing** (H: "you are mixing typing and drawing" — no type field; keyboard edits go into the line input below the strip). Any change drops the AI check and re-checks the line automatically.
     - **The character as it looks in the photo** is shown in the drawing sheet only (H, v89: no thumbnail in the picker's "Replace with" row — it crowded the candidates). The sheet's view starts at `charBox(sg,k,i)` — **not** at Tesseract's symbol box: those drift along a Chinese line (measured on the test sign: from the third character on, a box covers the right half of one character and the left half of the next; H saw a blank patch). The line's overall extent and the box heights are right, so the span from the first x0 to the last x1 is split evenly among the characters (signs are monospaced; a line mixing Latin or digits will be less precise) and the size comes from the median box height.
     - **Level 1, candidates:** while the AI is live it is asked at once for that position (`aiCharAlternatives`, a small separate text request; green-ringed buttons first); what the other readings saw at that position comes next (`altCharsAt`, v96), then the dictionary adds characters that form a word with the neighbours (`charCandidates`: 2–4-character words fitting around the position, longer context first, then by how common the character is, `CHARFREQ`); or **Remove** it. No insert mode: a missing character is typed into the line input (an "add a character at any place" picker was built in v91 and taken out again in v92 on H's request — "not convinced").
     - **Level 2, Not here? Draw it** (when the right one is missing): a **full-screen drawing sheet** (`openDrawSheet`, `.drawsheet` fixed over everything, `body.noscroll`) with **two equal squares** (H: "symmetrical, same size as the drawing field"): the photo character on top (`attachRefView`: a 600 px square view of `sg.img` that starts at `charBox` × 1.5 and is **moved with one finger and zoomed with two** — or the mouse wheel —, photo edges padded in `--fill`; H: "why can't I just drag the image into position and enlarge it?" — the automatic crop is a guess, the hand corrects it) and the pad below (faint 田字格, pointer events); `fitRef` gives both one side = min(sheet width, half the height left after heading, status, result row and buttons), ≥ 160 px (~295 px on a 844 px viewport), refit on resize; Undo/Clear and a red **Done** at the bottom.
     - **Reading the strokes:** **nothing is read until Done is tapped** — no timer (H: an inline pad below the fold went unseen, and reading after every stroke "takes it without me confirming"). On Done the strokes are rendered black on white at ~80 px and read by the same Tesseract model (`recognizeStrokes`: the model gives no per-symbol alternatives, so three readings are combined — single-word PSM 8 reads hand strokes far better than single-character PSM 10 — ranked by confidence, then what fits the neighbours first); the results appear as big buttons under the pad; tapping one replaces the character and closes the sheet; Clear to draw again. Nothing downloaded, offline, nothing leaves the device; a print-trained model on handwriting, so clean strokes matter — field accuracy unknown.
4. **Meaning priority** (H's rule): online AI → phrasebook → offline model → word gloss. `aiLive()` = key set + auto on + `navigator.onLine`. While the AI is live the offline model is not run and Save waits for the running AI check (`sg.aiPromise`); if the call fails, `sg.aiErr` is set and the offline model takes over; with neither, the card gets the gloss and `mt.pending:true` and is completed by the post-save auto run when online.
5. **After saving:** the note under the photo says what was saved and where the meaning came from (the **Next photo** button of v39–v93 is gone — H, v94; the camera tab's own button is enough). Photos picked from the album that are still waiting keep their CROP button below. `QSCARD[id]` remembers the card; if the AI answers later, the green suggestion box with **Accept / Dismiss** appears right there.

### Online AI review (T3, opt-in, text only)
- **Setup:** More → Online AI review → **provider** (`AI_PROVIDERS`: DeepSeek default — **field-verified from the phone, no VPN** —, Qwen/Alibaba Bailian, GLM/Zhipu, Claude, custom OpenAI-style base URL; the Chinese ones take WeChat Pay/Alipay, H could not buy Anthropic credit from China), API key (settings store, device only, never exported), model (defaults `deepseek-chat`, `qwen-plus`, `glm-4-flash`, `claude-sonnet-5`), checkbox "Check every new card with the AI automatically" (default on). Settings: `aiProvider`, `aiBase`, `aiKey`, `aiModel`, `aiAuto`.
- **Garbage is garbage (v95):** the prompt tells the model that the OCR text may be unreadable and asks for `bad:true` then — keep `zh`, empty `m`, say so in the note, **never replace an unreadable text with a guess** (H's Yakult logo: the AI turned 习/汶… into 了 "completed action marker" and waved 夸交多 / 一汰 / 洛相本 through as checked). A bad answer changes nothing in the Read preview: red head "The AI cannot make sense of this reading — frame the text tightly and read again, or fix the characters.", the fields keep the reading's own pinyin and gloss, the review flag is pre-ticked with the note "AI: the reading looks wrong", Save stores `mt.suspect` and unverified; after saving, a bad suggestion shows as "AI: this text looks misread" with **⚑ Flag for review** (`aiFlag`) instead of Accept, and Accept-all flags such cards.
- **Request:** OpenAI-style `POST {base}/chat/completions` with `Authorization: Bearer` (system + user message, temperature 0), or `api.anthropic.com/v1/messages` for Claude (`anthropic-dangerous-direct-browser-access`). Payload per card: hanzi, pinyin, meaning, note, why (flagged / uncertain reading / pending), gloss, alt (the other readings, v96) — **never photos**. Reply = JSON array `[{c,zh,p,m,note,ok,bad}]`. Cost ≈ 400 input + 100 output tokens per card, well under ¥0.01 at DeepSeek; a hundred cards a day ≈ ¥0.10–0.20.
- **Where it acts:** in the Read preview before saving (result shown in green, Save stores it verified); after saving as a suggestion stored `ai:{zh,p,m,note,ok,at,model}` with **Accept** (applies pinyin/meaning, renames the card if the text was fixed via `applyCardUpdate`, clears flag and `suspect`, `mt.src:"llm",verified:true`) or **Dismiss** — on the study back, in the card detail, under the photo, and via the **"N AI suggestions waiting — Accept all"** bar on the Cards tab; in the **Edit form** ("Ask AI to check text, pinyin and meaning" fills the fields in place, stored on Save). **Ask AI about N cards** in More sends the whole queue (`aiQueue()` = flagged + uncertain + pending) in one request; the auto run (`aiAuto`, `aiAutoSoon` 1.5 s after a save, and on `online`) does the same for pending/uncertain cards.

### Offline translation (T2, opt-in)
Firefox Translations `zh-Hans→en` v2.0a2 (Mozilla, MPL-2.0; model 44 MB + shortlist 4.7 MB + vocab 0.7 MB gzipped = 50 MB) in the Bergamot WASM engine (`vendor/nmt/translator-worker.js` + `bergamot-translator-worker.{js,wasm}`, npm `@browsermt/bergamot-translator` 0.4.9, no SharedArrayBuffer). Fetched by the **GitHub Action "Fetch zh→en translation model"** (workflow_dispatch) from **Mozilla Remote Settings** (the source Firefox uses; the Git LFS objects on GitHub are gone, 410; the build sandbox cannot reach Mozilla) — it writes `nmt-model.json` (sizes, sha256), bumps both version markers and pushes branch `nmt-model`; Actions may not open PRs here, so the PR is opened by hand. Enable under More → Translation (size prompt; files cached in `zt-ocr-v1`), setting `nmt`; used **only without a connection** once the AI is set up. `signMeaning(lines)` → `{m, src: phrasebook|nmt|gloss, pending}`; "Translate pending" in More completes gloss-only cards. Verified on the build machine (~40 ms per sentence after load), **not yet timed on the Xiaomi** — the More row shows "loaded in N s, last translation N s" (`NMT.loadMs`, `NMT.lastMs`) for that field test.

### Learning and the library
- **Learn:** SM-2 light; progress rows are `{c, interval, ease, due, reps, fails, last}` (`last` = day of the last review); every review day is appended to setting `days` (max 400) for the **streak**; `learnStats()` → cards learned / reviewed this week / streak, shown on the All-clear screen and in More → Progress — `fails` counts consecutive "again" (hard keeps it, good/easy reset it) and at `LEECH_FAILS` = 4 the card is **flagged automatically** ("failed N times in a row — check text, meaning and photo"): a leech is usually a bad card, not a bad memory. Front = photo (crop) above the text box (see **Line breaks** and Design). No tag row, no Reveal button — **tapping the character toggles the back on and off; tapping the photo swaps the crop for the whole photo and back** (`S.fullPic`, `frontPic`, reset on the next card). Back = pinyin with a **speaker button** (the phone's own zh-CN voice via `speechSynthesis`, nothing downloaded, shown only when such a voice exists — `ttsVoice()`, voices arrive asynchronously on Android), meaning, a **parts row** — the card's dictionary words as buttons (from `gloss`/`segs`/`seg`; a single-word card shows its characters instead), tap one for pinyin and meaning, a multi-character word then offers its characters as a sub-row (`cardParts`/`charsHTML`/`charInfo`, dictionary loaded on first tap, offline; CC-CEDICT clutter like `[Tian1 jin1 shi4]` and `CL:` stripped by `cleanSense`). No word or gloss tables on the back (H: redundant) —, (context word), then **Again / Hard / Good / Easy** with interval preview (after grading the next card appears at the top of the screen), and underneath **⚑ Flag for review** and **✎ Edit** (`S.editFrom="study"`, Back/Save return to the revealed card). No example sentences (H's decision). Empty deck → "No cards yet" + Take a photo.
- **Line breaks — the card text follows the photo, automatically (H's rule):** the front never wraps on its own; up to 3 characters sit in the square reticle, longer words get a wide box and the font is fitted to the longest line (`frontLines`, `frontBox`). Where the lines come from: the reader keeps Tesseract's lines (sign cards: `c` with `\n`; word cards: a `"\n"` token in `seg`, chars carry `ln`); the AI's answer is re-cut to those lines when the model drops the `\n` (`recutLines`: old break offsets, so a corrected character keeps them and an added one only lengthens the last line); a rename keeps them the same way (`segWithBreaks`); the Edit form shows word cards with one strip and one line input per photo line (the hint sentence about it was confusing and is gone). Cards without any line data (before v39) get their lines **estimated once at boot** from the crop's shape (`autoBreaks`: L ≈ √(n·h/w), cut at word boundaries, stored with `lb:"auto"`); cards with real line data are `lb:"photo"` and never touched.
- **Cards:** every card newest first with a 104×58 thumbnail of the **crop** (`img`; the whole photo only for cards without one — H, v86, reversing v54; `object-fit: contain`) or a glyph tile, pinyin, meaning, ⚑ review / green AI pill and the real SRS status (`due`, `in N d`); search over hanzi, pinyin, meanings, context words and flag notes; chips **⚑ Flagged (N)** (flag only), **AI (N)** (waiting suggestions, shown only when there are any) and **Unverified (N)**; **+ New** opens the Add form.
- **Card detail:** front + back preview with the study screen's taps (H: "enable all test functions in the Cards preview" — tap the character to hide and show the answer, `S.detailHide`; tap the photo for the whole picture, `S.fullPic`; both reset on opening a card and on ← Cards; speaker and parts row as on the back), SRS stats, **Test this card** (studies that card and then continues down the list, newest first; ← Cards stops and restores the session queue), **Edit**, **⚑ Flag / Clear flag**, **Delete**.
- **Edit form:** the text is edited **like the Read preview** (H): a character strip per line with the picker and the drawing sheet (`SIGN["editN"]` carries the lines and the card's crop as the photo reference; `sg.onChange` lets the form own the re-render and trigger its AI button), the line input below the strip, pinyin following the text unless edited by hand, meaning — both full-width fields that **grow with their text** instead of cutting it off (`textarea.grow`, `autoGrow`/`wireGrow`, also in the Add form; read back as one line — H) —, the image field showing the **crop itself** (not the whole-photo thumbnail — H) with removal, flag; the context-word fields show only on cards that already have one; the AI button fills text, pinyin and meaning in place; **Delete card** at the bottom (H) — from the study back the session goes on with the next card, otherwise back to the list. **The Chinese text itself** may change: progress, images and queue entries move to the new key via `applyCardUpdate`; pinyin/segmentation/gloss are recomputed unless pinyin was edited by hand; editing marks `verified`, clears `pending` and `suspect`.
- **Review flag:** `flag:true` / `flagNote` on the card; included in export. More → **Flagged cards → Show** opens the Cards list with the Flagged filter on; **Share** sends a plain-text list via the share sheet (`.txt`) for a teacher.
- **Export/import** (More): progress + cards as JSON, `zeichentrainer-YYYY-MM-DD.json.txt` via the share sheet; import validates by content and upserts, keeping local images. The Export row shows **"Last export: N days ago"** (setting `lastExport`), red after 30 days or when never exported — the cards exist only on this phone. **Photos** row: inbox count, and a **Delete N** button for photos older than 30 days that already became a card (cards carry `shot:<inbox id>`; the cards keep their own crop). **Reset** wipes cards, progress and photos. **Diagnostics** (v93, H: "crop draws, nothing happens afterwards" could not be reproduced in Chromium — the phone has to tell): the last 20 uncaught errors and unhandled rejections (`ERRLOG`, `logErr`, persisted in setting `errlog`), the steps of the last reading with their times (`READLOG`) and its competing passes (`LAST_READ.passes`), plus page/script version, online state, AI provider (never the key), service-worker presence and user agent — **Show** expands the text in place (`.diag`), **Share** sends it as a `.txt` via the share sheet (`shareDiag`; a third share-sheet dance on purpose, the two others keep their own texts).

### Field lessons that shaped the UI (keep)
- **Flow and UI:** No tab switching for the AI (v31/v32) · button always visible, never replaced by the auto result (v32) · one action after framing (v39) · plain words instead of jargon and `·` shorthand (v25/v39) · three top-bar pills overflow at 390 px, so Deck only shows off the Learn tab (v25) · Chrome/Android does not repaint after the camera until touched (v34) · the card text keeps the photo's lines, a horizontal word is one line (v42) · reading starts by itself after the frame is released, no Read tap (v47) · the crop frame stays on the photo until Save (v50) · after grading the next card must start at the top of the screen (v53) · two taps on the front: photo = whole picture, character = answer (v55) · the static HTML must already match the first render (Deck pill hidden in index.html), otherwise the top bar jumps at load (v57) · `html` needs the page background, a `color-scheme` and an inline fallback in index.html, otherwise a line in the wrong colour flashes above the page at load (v58, kept for light and dark in v82).
- **Storage, deploys and network:** Android shares `.txt` but not `.json` (v3) · a cached 404 poisoned the dictionary once (SW caches only OK responses) · status text written into a DOM node captured before a re-render disappears silently — keep status in state and re-query (v47) · github.io is unreliable from China, jsDelivr is not (v48/v49) · the reader's files must be reachable without the VPN too, hence the mirror fallback for `vendor/` (v56) · a new worker must fetch the shell past the browser's HTTP cache, or rapid deploys leave the old app under a new cache name (v71) · the script carries its own version and checks it against the page's label at boot, so a mixed shell repairs itself (v72) · "the frame draws, nothing happens afterwards" on v92 was not reproducible in Chromium and vanished with the v93 update (VPN on) — cause never seen, most likely a mixed shell from the mirror, which caches each file on its own; first ask for More → Diagnostics before touching the reader (v93).
- **The reader:** a tilted frame is straightened before reading — Tesseract fails beyond a few degrees (v59) · Tesseract's per-symbol boxes are not to be trusted for Chinese: the heights are right, the horizontal ends drift, stop early on the last character or lose it altogether — take the line's ends from the image columns (v69/v75) · **what the reader sees must be a JPEG** — this Tesseract build misreads large canvas PNGs and WebPs (same pixels: JPEG 0.95 → the full line at 99 %, PNG → one character); intermediates stay PNG so only the last step is lossy — two JPEG generations in a row lost the line too (`READ_JPEG`, `deskewBlob` always hands back a JPEG, `jpegOf` re-encodes card images to JPEG 0.85 at save) · the reader is chaotic on large text: the same clean crop reads perfectly at one scale and as garbage at another, so read at several scales and let the readings compete (v73) · light text on a strong colour defeats the reader's own thresholding — a binarised copy reads it (v77) · shading (a curved bottle) defeats every brightness threshold, a chromaticity copy does not care (v95) · the score must not reward a soup of short lines, and a band only one pass saw or that is tiny beside the others is decoration (v95) · the AI must be allowed to say "unreadable" — asked only to correct, it invents (v95) · a traditional logo needs the traditional model, the simplified one can only approximate (v96) · confidence must weigh more than character count, and a text several passes agree on beats a lone near-equal score (v96) · the AI sees every competing reading, not just the winner (v96).
- **Fixing a character and drawing:** one misread character is fixed by tapping it, not by retyping the line; the AI is asked first, the dictionary is the fallback (v61) · candidates first, drawing as the second level when the right character is missing; the photo character stays in view while drawing, because the system keyboard's handwriting pad hid it (v62) · the Tesseract worker has **one** logger: progress goes to `_ocrLog`, the handler of the running job, never to the status closure of whoever created the worker — the pad's reading once wrote "recognizing … 100%" into the photo's box and wiped the editor (v63) · action rows in the picker are flex (`.ckacts`), not the auto-fit grid, which broke the pad's buttons (v63) · the picker is two levels only, suggestions then drawing; no type field in it (v64) · the word-picking overlay (pinyin boxes on the photo, selection bar) went unused once the AI-first preview existed — removed with its Split into words button (v91) · inserting a character through the picker (+ before / + after, a trailing + on the strip) did not convince H — removed again in v92; the line input covers it · the drawing pad is a full-screen sheet with a Done button — an inline pad below the fold went unseen, and reading after each stroke felt like being cut off (v65) · an automatic crop is a guess — let the hand correct it (drag/pinch on the photo square) (v70) · small black-on-white pad images are indifferent to PNG/JPEG (measured) (v73).

## Hard constraints (learned in the field — do not violate)
1. **No external dependencies / CDNs.** Must run offline and behind the GFW. System CJK fonts only. Libraries and data go into `vendor/` (licenses in `vendor/LICENSES.txt`).
2. **All paths relative** (`./…`). The app lives under a subpath (`/zeichentrainer/`).
3. **Persistence only via IndexedDB** (do not use localStorage/sessionStorage). `navigator.storage.persist()` is requested — Xiaomi/MIUI aggressively evicts storage of non-installed sites. The SW must **never cache non-OK responses** — a 404 cached during a deploy window poisoned the dictionary permanently once; `loadDict` additionally heals such entries.
4. **Phone-only deploys:** H uses GitHub only in the phone browser. Ship changes as small, clearly described PRs. As few files as possible; generate/commit binaries (icons, vendor data) in the repo, never ask H to upload them.
5. **Privacy rule:** Confidential text (real contracts, suppliers, company internals) must **never** end up in the public deck or repo. General vocabulary only. Photos and card images stay on the device.
6. **Files leave the device only via the share sheet:** programmatic blob downloads (`<a download>`) are silently blocked by Android/MIUI. Use `navigator.share` with a file — and Chrome/Android only shares whitelisted types (`.txt`/`.csv`/images/PDF yes, **`.json` no**), hence the `.json.txt` extension with `text/plain`.

## Deck format
No built-in deck; all cards live in IndexedDB `custom`. Schema:
```
{ c:"坚果", p:"jiānguǒ", m:"nuts", t:"Custom", at:1756800000000,   // t: Custom | Sign · at: creation time
  seg:["坚果","\n","供应"],           // optional: word boundaries + photo line breaks for the front
  lb:"photo"|"auto",                    // where the line breaks came from (auto = estimated from the crop's shape)
  w:"…", wp:"…", wm:"…",              // optional: context word + pinyin + meaning
  shot:"shot_1756800000000",           // inbox photo the card came from (photo cleanup)
  mt:{ src:"llm"|"dict"|"phrasebook"|"nmt"|"gloss", verified:true|false, pending:false, suspect:"reason" },
  flag:true, flagNote:"…",            // review flag
  alts:["养兴多","义乐多"],               // optional: the other readings of the photo (v96), for the AI check
  ai:{ zh, p, m, note, ok, bad, at, model } // a waiting AI suggestion (bad: the AI calls the text OCR garbage)
}
```
Sign cards add `kind:"sign"`, `segs` (per line) and `gloss` (`[{w,p,m}]`, feeds the parts row); word cards from the Read path carry `gloss` too; any card saved from a photo carries `img` (crop) and `imgFull` (whole photo: list thumbnail and the front toggle — not repeated on the back, H) — blobs, local only, never exported.
Rules: **Pinyin only verified, with correct tones — never guess.** Meanings in English. Dictionary/phrasebook prefills are `verified:false` until a human or the AI checked them; when unsure, flag instead of inventing.
New words come from photos (menus, signs, packaging).

## Testing
No test files in the repo. A full audit (dead code, unused CSS, duplicated helpers, stale comments) was done at v81; the mechanical scan for unreferenced functions, constants and CSS classes, and the check that every identifier named here exists in the code, are worth repeating after larger changes.
