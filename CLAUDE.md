## What this is
Chinese character trainer for adults (spaced repetition), a reinterpretation of 悟空识字 without the kids' aesthetic.
User: H, product manager, Beijing, **phone-only (Android/Xiaomi, Chrome, VPN), no computer**.
UI language: English. Learning content: Chinese + pinyin + English meaning.

## Live deployment
- Repo: `henglicam/zeichentrainer` · GitHub Pages, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
- URL: `https://henglicam.github.io/zeichentrainer/`
- Push to `main` → Pages rebuilds automatically (~1–2 min). `index.html` must stay in the repo root.

### The flow (AI-first — "nobody wants to see OCR if not necessary")
1. **Camera:** `<input type="file" capture="environment">` (Take photo) or `<input type="file" multiple>` (**From album** — several at once, all land in the inbox, the first opens in crop mode; `addPhoto` per file) — or **a screenshot shared to the app from another app** (v163, H: "a fast and easy function to translate screenshots": the manifest's `share_target` posts the files to `./share`, the worker's `shareIn` parks them in the cache `zt-share` and answers with a redirect to `./?share=1`, the page's `takeShared` moves them into the inbox through `importPhotos` — the same path as the album, the first in crop mode; the installed app only, a browser tab has no share target; Chrome picks the new manifest up on a later launch, so the entry in the share sheet may take a day or a re-install — not yet field-checked) → the Read preview has a **"Done, no card"** link beside Cancel (`data-signdone`, v163) that discards the photo when only the translation was wanted → the photo is normalized (EXIF baked in, max 1600 px) and stored in `inbox`. Measured on a striped, ribboned composite: garbage at 86 % → 本区域禁止违规 at 99 %; the 12° sign still reads both lines; H's granite sign 等候区 (yellow paint in carved characters on speckled stone) reads at 93 % once the fallbacks run (v100, 44 passes, ~30 s in headless Chromium); the Yakult screenshots read 5菌型乳酸菌乳饮品 (the faded 活 at the bottle's edge becomes a stray 5) and, since v99, 养乐多 by dictionary consensus (the passes themselves give 养兴多 / 义乐多 / 和准兴多 — 楽 is a Japanese glyph neither model knows, and the 和 is the oval's left arc). Diagnostics: `region.passes` (the competition) and `region.tightBlob` (the tight crop).

The pad's small black-on-white images are indifferent (measured).

- **Setup:** More → Online AI review → **provider** (`AI_PROVIDERS`: DeepSeek default — **field-verified from the phone, no VPN** —, Qwen/Alibaba Bailian, GLM/Zhipu, Claude, custom OpenAI-style base URL; the Chinese ones take WeChat Pay/Alipay, H could not buy Anthropic credit from China), API key (settings store, device only, never exported), model (defaults `deepseek-chat`, `qwen-plus`, `glm-4-flash`, `claude-sonnet-5`), checkbox "Check every new card with the AI automatically" (default on). - **Answers are checked (v97):** every `zh` is normalised to simplified characters (`t2s`, v103 — told that the sign is traditional, the model answered with traditional `zh`, and 養樂多 has no dictionary entry, so the meaning lost "Yakult"; a traditional `zh` becomes `zht` when the model gave none; the prompt says "always simplified"); a meaning without Latin letters (the model once echoed the Chinese text as `m`) is dropped and logged, the fields keep the reading's own gloss; the prompt says "in English only" and that the text is usually a sign, menu item, product name or brand — "when the readings circle around a well-known brand, zh is that name"; before calling a text bad it must try the `alt` readings and answer with the plausible one (v98 — on the phone it called the soup garbage, correctly, and left 养浴多 / 次乐多 / 开乐多 unused).
- **A clearly read character is not the AI's to change (v143):** H's bicycle sticker 减震单车 — every pass read 减 at 98 %, DeepSeek answered 共享单车 "shared bicycle", a common phrase that is not the sign, and the preview swapped the right 减 for 共 ("Zunächst wird der erste character richtig angezeigt und dann jedoch zu einem falschen geändert"). `aiSettled(sg,lines,zh)`: when the answer keeps the line count and each line's length, every changed position must be open — the reading's confidence there under `AI_SETTLED` (90), or another reading of the same line length shows the AI's character at that position (养兴多 → 养乐多 with 义乐多 among the alts, 和 → 活 with the 活菌型… alt). A settled position blocks the whole answer: the reading stays, `sg.ai={zh:c,proposed,kept}`, the fields keep the reading's own pinyin and gloss, the remark reads "The AI suggested 共享单车, but 减 was read clearly, so the reading stays. Meaning composed word by word, unverified.", Save stores the card unverified with its gloss (the post-save auto run may then offer the AI's text as a suggestion with Accept — a suggestion, not a silent replacement). A line edited by hand before the check has no confidences left and is never guarded; an answer of another length (专业冰矫正 → 专业脊柱矫正) is not guarded; the guard lives in the Read preview only (saved cards carry no confidences). Field-confirmed with v144 on the sticker (H: "OK. Works.").
- **Garbage is garbage (v95):** the prompt tells the model that the OCR text may be unreadable and asks for `bad:true` then — keep `zh`, empty `m`, say so in the note, **never replace an unreadable text with a guess** (H's Yakult logo: the AI turned 习/汶… into 了 "completed action marker" and waved 夸交多 / 一汰 / 洛相本 through as checked). A bad answer changes nothing in the Read preview: red head "This reading looks wrong — frame the text tightly and read again, or fix the characters.", the fields keep the reading's own pinyin and gloss, the review flag is pre-ticked with the note "the reading looks wrong", Save stores `mt.suspect` ("the text looks misread") and unverified; after saving, a bad suggestion shows as "AI: this text looks misread" with **⚑ Flag for review** (`aiFlag`) instead of Accept, and Accept-all flags such cards.
- **Request:** OpenAI-style `POST {base}/chat/completions` with `Authorization: Bearer` (system + user message, temperature 0), or `api.anthropic.com/v1/messages` for Claude (`anthropic-dangerous-direct-browser-access`). Reply = JSON array `[{c,zh,zht,p,m,note,ok,bad}]` (`zht` only for `script:"traditional"`). - **Where it acts:** in the Read preview before saving (result shown in green, Save stores it verified); after saving as a suggestion stored `ai:{zh,p,m,note,ok,at,model}` with **Accept** (applies pinyin/meaning, fixes the text in place via `applyCardUpdate`, clears flag and `suspect`, `mt.src:"llm",verified:true`) or **Dismiss** — on the study back, in the card detail, under the photo, and via the **"N AI suggestions waiting — Accept all"** bar on the Cards tab; in the **Edit form** ("Ask AI to check text, pinyin and meaning" fills the fields in place, stored on Save). **Ask AI about N cards** in More sends the whole queue (`aiQueue()` = flagged + uncertain + pending) in one request; the auto run (`aiAuto`, `aiAutoSoon` 1.5 s after a save, and on `online`) does the same for pending/uncertain cards.

## Hard constraints (learned in the field — do not violate)
1. **No external dependencies / CDNs.** Must run offline and behind the GFW. System CJK fonts only. Libraries and data go into `vendor/` (licenses in `vendor/LICENSES.txt`).
2. **All paths relative** (`./…`). The app lives under a subpath (`/zeichentrainer/`).
3. **Persistence only via IndexedDB** (do not use localStorage/sessionStorage). `navigator.storage.persist()` is requested — Xiaomi/MIUI aggressively evicts storage of non-installed sites. The SW must **never cache non-OK responses** — a 404 cached during a deploy window poisoned the dictionary permanently once; `loadDict` additionally heals such entries.
4. **Phone-only deploys:** H uses GitHub only in the phone browser. Ship changes as small, clearly described PRs. As few files as possible; generate/commit binaries (icons, vendor data) in the repo, never ask H to upload them.
5. **Privacy rule:** Confidential text (real contracts, suppliers, company internals) must **never** end up in the public deck or repo. General vocabulary only. Photos and card images stay on the device.
6. **Files leave the device only via the share sheet:** programmatic blob downloads (`<a download>`) are silently blocked by Android/MIUI. Use `navigator.share` with a file — and Chrome/Android only shares whitelisted types (`.txt`/`.csv`/images/PDF yes, **`.json` no**), hence the `.json.txt` extension with `text/plain`.

## Deck format
No built-in deck; all cards live in IndexedDB `custom`. Schema:
```
{ id:"坚果", c:"坚果", p:"jiānguǒ", m:"nuts", t:"Custom", at:1756800000000,   // id: the key (text while free, else 坚果#<at>) · t: Custom | Sign · at: creation time
  tags:["Chinese class","HSK 3"],     // optional: H's own labels (v133) — the Cards filter and the Learn filter
  seg:["坚果","\n","供应"],           // optional: word boundaries + photo line breaks for the front
  lb:"photo"|"auto",                    // where the line breaks came from (auto = estimated from the crop's shape)
  w:"…", wp:"…", wm:"…",              // optional: context word + pinyin + meaning
  shot:"shot_1756800000000",           // inbox photo the card came from (photo cleanup)
  mt:{ src:"llm"|"dict"|"phrasebook"|"nmt"|"gloss", verified:true|false, pending:false, suspect:"reason" },
  flag:true, flagNote:"…",            // review flag
  alts:["养兴多","义乐多"],               // optional: the other readings of the photo (v96), for the AI check
  trad:"養樂多",                          // optional: the text as it stands on the photo, in traditional characters (v101); shown on the front, c stays simplified
  ai:{ zh, p, m, note, ok, bad, at, model } // a waiting AI suggestion (bad: the AI calls the text OCR garbage)
}
```
Sign cards add `kind:"sign"`, `segs` (per line) and `gloss` (`[{w,p,m}]`, feeds the parts row); word cards from the Read path carry `gloss` too; any card saved from a photo carries `img` (crop) and `imgFull` (whole photo: list thumbnail and the front toggle — not repeated on the back, H) — blobs, local only, never exported.
Rules: **Pinyin only verified, with correct tones — never guess.** Meanings in English. Dictionary/phrasebook prefills are `verified:false` until a human or the AI checked them; when unsure, flag instead of inventing.
New words come from photos (menus, signs, packaging).

## Working with H — the how-to rules (v128, agreed after the four-corner episode)
**What H sends.** One request per message: a sentence, and a screenshot when it is about a screen. When the reader is wrong, More → Diagnostics → Share with it — the phone is the only place the reader can be watched from. "Leave it as it was" means: revert to the previous behaviour, no discussion.

**What Claude does with a request.**
1. **Restate before building.** One sentence: what changes for H on the phone. Two readings that lead to different work → one question, not five. Otherwise no questions.
2. **Size gate.** Wording, layout, a rule in the reader, a bug: build at once. **Anything that changes how H handles the app** (a new gesture, a new field or control, a new screen, a changed flow) is described first in three lines — what H does, what H sees, what it costs — and waits for "go". A "go" is for that description; if the built thing feels different, H says so and it is reverted in the same session.
3. **Only the ask.** No "while I'm at it" changes. Consistency passes, cleanups and refactors only when H asks for them.
4. **Field first.** A reader change is judged by the phone, not by the harness. Every claim about the phone is either seen on the phone (screenshot, diagnostics) or marked "not yet field-checked".
5. **One request, one PR, one version.** Every PR: the three version markers bumped, the suites that touch the change run, CLAUDE.md updated in the same PR, merged by Claude, branch reset onto main. A revert is a PR like any other, with its own version.
6. **CLAUDE.md is the memory.** Decisions, rejected features and field lessons go in with the reason; "Zieh CLAUDE.md nach" means consolidate into the current state, not a version log. What H rejected is not brought back unasked.
7. **Report short, in English.** What changed, what was verified and where, what is open. Honesty over confidence: "I don't know" beats a guess; limits are named.
8. **Wording and design.** Sentence case, plain words, no jargon (no "OCR"), no "·" shorthand; iOS-style tokens; pinyin in the UI font; the rules in Design and Hard constraints are not up for interpretation.
- Build-first. Physical reality beats spec.
- On every change: confirm the app still runs with no network dependencies and relative paths.
