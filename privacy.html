Privacy policy

识字 Zeichentrainer · Last updated 10 September 2026

识字 Zeichentrainer is a Chinese character trainer. It is built so that your cards and photos stay on your device. Two things it sends by itself, and you can switch off both: a small daily count of how much the app is used, and — to have the pinyin and the meaning checked — the text of a new card, with the framed part of a photo when the reader could not read it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in the app — your cards, your photos, the cropped card images, your learning progress, and your settings — is stored only on your own device, in the browser's local database. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. We cannot see, collect, or access any of your data.

What leaves your device

  • Text recognition and handwriting recognition run entirely on the device. Nothing is uploaded for them. The two exceptions, both under the one switch described below, are the AI check of a card's text and the framed area of a photo the reader could not read.
  • Usage counts (on by default, can be switched off under More → Usage sharing). Once a day, while online, the app sends anonymous usage counts to the app's owner: a random installation id, the app version, the phone model, the language your phone is set to, how many days the app was used, your day streak, the number of app opens, reviews, cards learned, created and deleted, how many cards, photos and tags you hold, AI calls with their token counts, which AI provider and model did the work, whether the app is installed on the home screen, and the app's last error messages (what failed and the message, with any Chinese characters removed). No card text, no photos, no API key, no name, no account, no location. The counts are stored in a database table (Supabase) that only the app's owner can read, to see how the app is used. Switching the setting off stops the sending at once.
  • Online AI review (on by default, can be switched off under More → Online AI review). When a card is made or changed, the app sends the text of that card — the characters, pinyin, meaning, and note, never photos — to an AI provider (for example DeepSeek, Alibaba, Zhipu, or Anthropic) to have the pinyin and the meaning checked. That request is between your device and the provider and is governed by that provider's privacy policy. If you enter an API key of your own it is used instead, and it is stored only on your device. A device without a key of its own — the normal case — sends these requests through a small relay run by the app's owner (a Supabase edge function), which adds the owner's key, forwards the request to the provider, and stores nothing but a daily count per installation; the owner's key never reaches the device.
  • The framed area of a photo (on by default, needs a provider whose model reads pictures). When the on-device reader cannot read the text, the app sends the part of the photo it framed — reduced to at most 800 pixels — to a provider whose model reads pictures (Alibaba's Qwen, Zhipu's GLM, or Anthropic's Claude); through the owner's relay that is Qwen, so this works without a key of your own. If the provider answers that the framed part holds no Chinese text, or finds the text against its edge, the app opens the frame to the whole photo and asks once more — in those two cases the whole photo is sent, again reduced to at most 800 pixels. The same switch covers this and the card check above: untick it under More → Online AI review and no picture and no card text ever leaves the device.
  • Downloads. The app fetches its own files (the app itself, recognition data, the optional translation model) from its hosting (GitHub Pages) or a public mirror (jsDelivr). These are ordinary web requests; like any web server, the hosts may log standard technical data such as your IP address. We do not receive or store any of it.
  • Feedback (only when you send it). A message you write under More → Feedback goes to the app's owner together with the app version and the same random installation id as the usage counts. It carries no name, no cards and no photos unless you type them.
  • Exports and sharing. Your data leaves the device only when you choose to export or share it through your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes.

Analytics and tracking

Apart from the daily usage counts described above, the app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party scripts. The counts carry a random installation id, not your identity, and cannot be linked to you.

Deleting your data

Your data belongs to you and lives on your device. You can delete individual cards and photos in the app, wipe everything under More → Start over, or remove all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its site data.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool for adults. It does not knowingly collect any personal information — from children or anyone else.
