Privacy policy

识字 Zeichentrainer · Last updated 4 September 2026

识字 Zeichentrainer is a Chinese character trainer. It is built so that your cards and photos stay on your device. The only thing it sends on its own is a small daily count of how much the app is used, which you can switch off.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in the app — your cards, your photos, the cropped card images, your learning progress, and your settings — is stored only on your own device, in the browser's local database. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. We cannot see, collect, or access any of your data.

What leaves your device

  • Photos and cards never leave your device on their own. Text recognition and handwriting recognition run entirely on the device.
  • Usage counts (on by default, can be switched off under More → Usage sharing). Once a day, while online, the app sends anonymous usage counts to the app's owner: a random installation id, the app version, the phone model, how many days the app was used, the number of app opens, reviews, cards learned and created, and AI calls with their token counts. No card text, no photos, no API key, no name, no account, no location. The counts are stored in a database table (Supabase) that only the app's owner can read, to see how the app is used. Switching the setting off stops the sending at once.
  • Online AI review (optional, off by default). If you enable it and enter your own API key, the app sends the text of a card — the characters, pinyin, meaning, and note, never photos — to the AI provider you chose (for example DeepSeek, Alibaba, Zhipu, or Anthropic). That request is between your device and the provider and is governed by that provider's privacy policy. Your API key is stored only on your device. A device without a key of its own sends these requests through a small relay run by the app's owner (a Supabase edge function), which adds the owner's key, forwards the request to the provider, and stores nothing but a daily count per installation; the owner's key never reaches the device.
  • The framed area of a photo (optional, needs a provider that takes pictures). When you have set up a provider whose model reads pictures (Alibaba's Qwen, Zhipu's GLM, or Anthropic's Claude) and the switch "Send the framed area to the AI when the reading is weak" is on, the app sends the part of a photo you framed — reduced to at most 800 pixels, never the whole photo — to that provider when the on-device reader could not read it. You can switch this off under More → Online AI review; then no picture ever leaves the device.
  • Downloads. The app fetches its own files (the app itself, recognition data, the optional translation model) from its hosting (GitHub Pages) or a public mirror (jsDelivr). These are ordinary web requests; like any web server, the hosts may log standard technical data such as your IP address. We do not receive or store any of it.
  • Feedback (only when you send it). A message you write under More → Feedback goes to the app's owner together with the app version and the same random installation id as the usage counts. It carries no name, no cards and no photos unless you type them.
  • Exports and sharing. Your data leaves the device only when you choose to export or share it through your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes.

Analytics and tracking

Apart from the daily usage counts described above, the app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party scripts. The counts carry a random installation id, not your identity, and cannot be linked to you.

Deleting your data

Your data belongs to you and lives on your device. You can delete individual cards and photos in the app, wipe everything under More → Start over, or remove all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its site data.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool for adults. It does not knowingly collect any personal information — from children or anyone else.
